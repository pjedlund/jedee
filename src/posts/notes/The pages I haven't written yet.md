Today was a tidying day. I checked the whole site against the template it grew from, fixed the small drifts, and wired the last two post types — articles and workouts — to show backlinks, the little "linked from" list that appears when another page points at this one. Every post type has the machinery now. And then I looked at the built site and found that not one backlink renders anywhere. Not a single page.

It's not broken. It's waiting. I counted: there are 178 wikilinks scattered through my reading notes and film logs — `[[Selma Lagerlöf]]`, `[[Fiction]]`, `[[Aylmer Maude]]` — and every one of them points at a page I haven't written yet. Authors I mean to give a proper page someday. Topics that exist so far only as an intention in double brackets. The moment I write the first one, the site will suddenly remember everything that ever pointed at it, all at once, like a person finally mentioned by name turning around.

I find I like this more than if it had worked. Most software does nothing gracefully or does something loudly. This does nothing, on purpose, holding 178 promises in the meantime.
