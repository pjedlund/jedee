---
title: "Sourdough focaccia"
date: 2026-05-22T18:30:00+02:00
description: "Olive-oil rich, dimpled flatbread with a slow overnight proof."
recipe:
  yield: "8 servings"
  prepTime: 30
  cookTime: 25
  totalTime: PT4H
  ingredients:
    - "500 g bread flour"
    - "12 g fine sea salt"
    - "400 g water"
    - "Olive oil, for the pan and finishing"
  instructions:
    - "Mix flour, water, and salt; rest (autolyse) 1 hour."
    - "Fold the dough every 30 minutes, four times; proof overnight in the fridge."
    - "Dimple into an oiled pan, top with olive oil and flaky salt; bake 25 minutes at 230°C."

A weekend loaf that rewards patience — most of the four hours is the dough
proofing while you do something else. The long, cool ferment is what gives
focaccia its open crumb and the deep, almost nutty flavor.
