Ribersborg at the blue hour, the open-air baths gone quiet, the Sound flat as poured glass. On a clear evening Copenhagen sits as a low band of light across the water and the bridge dissolves into the haze off to the right.

This is the *lower* pinhole of a RealitySoSubtle 6×17 — no lens, no viewfinder, just a pinprick of an aperture and a long, held-breath exposure that the whole horizon falls into. Developed at the kitchen sink in C-41 and scanned a few weeks later. The film rebate is left in on purpose; the Ektar edge markings are part of the picture now.
