A warm, bright afternoon in the middle of July. The sea was almost flat — the waves arriving here are small, more a steady arrival than a crash, and they spread out thin and wide across the sand before sliding back.

I was there to look at the beach for a pinhole frame, one straight out at the horizon and one running along the shoreline, and set the recorder down while I worked. Partway through I turned the mic to point the other way, along the beach instead of at the water. The clip here is from the second position, which turned out to be the sheltered one — pointed at the sea the wind sat on the microphone the whole time.
