What the installed app looks like

Rendered from the real generated icons and the values the site actually emits. Two different colors drive the chrome: the manifest’s theme_color (#495464 slate) paints the splash and the recents card, but once a page loads the <meta name="theme-color"> tags in meta-info.njk take over — #F4F4F2 in light, #495464 in dark (was #bbbfca).

Every app icon is now the same orange tile. The manifest icons carry their own 22% corners because nothing masks them; apple-touch and maskable stay square edge to edge, since iOS and Android apply their own mask and baked corners would punch holes through it. Only the browser tab keeps the bare transparent mark.
