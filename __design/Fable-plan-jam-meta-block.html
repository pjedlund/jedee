Fable plan · rank 4 — closes the This Is My Jam arc

Jam metadata block: artist · album · year on every jam page

The last open job from the This Is My Jam import (Job 2C): the jam frontmatter is rich — artist, album, year, genres, sometimes a favorite track — but none of it shows on the page. This plan puts a quiet byline-style block under the title. The structure and a tasteful default styling get built; the final look stays yours to adjust.

Roughly this, under the title

Woo York · Chill Out 2 · 2015

Techno · Musik · Dance

Favorite track: Chasing the Dream

The favorite-track row exists on only 3 of 118 jams today — it appears only when set.

What shows, what deliberately doesn't

FieldTreatment
artist, album, yearThe byline row. Artist is stored as a wikilink and gets unwrapped by the (already committed) unwikilink filter.
genreA muted second row, dot-separated. Also wikilinks, also unwrapped.
favoriteTrack + URLA linked row, only when present.
source / odesliUrlStay invisible — the listen link remains hidden machine data; the YouTube embed covers playback. Decided earlier.
title, cover, embed, dateAlready on the page — not repeated.

Where it lives

  • Markup: inline in src/_layouts/jam.njk, right after the entry header (before the cover image).
  • Styling: a new per-page stylesheet src/assets/css/local/jam-meta.css, modeled on the photo pages' metadata block — small type, accent tokens, easy for you to restyle.

One thing before starting

The working tree currently holds the finished-but-uncommitted self-hosted YouTube poster work, which also touches jam.njk. That gets verified and committed as its own commit first, so the two pieces of work don't tangle.

Gotchas already scouted

  • Coverage is ragged: ~20 jams miss album/year/genre, one (the Boiler Room DJ mix) has no artist at all. Every piece must vanish cleanly — the separators are drawn by CSS, so a missing field never leaves a dangling "·".
  • The markup stays inline in the layout — moving it to a conditional include walks into a known Eleventy trap that silently blanks content.
  • Artist and genre names are not links — artist/genre pages don't exist (yet).
  • No new microformat classes by default; the hidden machine-readable data already satisfies parsers.

Done means

  • /jams/chasing-the-dream/ shows the full block including the favorite track; a This Is My Jam import shows the byline + genres; the DJ mix renders cleanly with no artist.
  • No visible listen/odesli links anywhere; the hidden machine data untouched.
  • Tests green, merged to main, waiting for your push — and Job 2C is finally closed.
